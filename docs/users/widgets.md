# The widgets

All 24 widgets are free. Each one shows only the settings it uses; [Settings](settings.md) explains each setting.

"Type" is the name a shortcode uses: `[ucwp_widget type="crypto-heatmap"]`.

## Tickers

| Widget | Type | What it shows | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coin Marquee | `coin-marquee-1` | A strip of coins and prices that scrolls across the page. | Number of coins, card width, widget width, speed, currency, live prices |
| Coin Marquee (2) | `coin-marquee-2` | The marquee in a second design, with your own choice of coins. | As above, plus coins |
| Coin Marquee (3) | `coin-marquee-3` | The marquee with a small price chart for each coin. | As above, plus days of price history and interval |
| Crypto Price Label | `crypto-price-picker-1` | One small card per coin with its price and 24-hour change, side by side or stacked. | Number of coins, layout, currency, live prices |
| Crypto Price Label (2), (3) | `crypto-price-picker-2`, `crypto-price-picker-3` | The price label in two more designs, with your own choice of coins. | As above, plus coins |
| Price Slider Widget | `price-slider-widget-1` | Coins and prices sliding past. | Number of coins, widget width, currency, speed, coins, live prices |
| Price Slider Widget (2) | `price-slider-widget-2` | A dark design, with buttons that choose which way it slides. | As above |

## Good to know

- **Coins left empty** means the top coins by market value, as many as **Number of coins** says.
- **Live prices** keep prices moving while the page is open. Market Overview and Trending Coins show figures from when
  the page loaded and have no live prices setting.
- Every widget follows the site-wide colours and accent colour unless its own **Colours** setting says otherwise.
